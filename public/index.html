How to read these numbers +

The collector retains seven days of network snapshots. When enabled, automatic collection is scheduled approximately every 30 minutes; its current state is shown above. Each bar shows the share of observations reporting “Good Service” on that London calendar date. Planned closures, delays and other reported notices count as other status; unknown status is excluded from the percentage.

This is a sample of line-wide reports, not train punctuality, journey reliability or the percentage of time a line operated normally. Overnight closures are included. Scheduled collection can be delayed or missed; missing observations are never treated as good service. Sample counts and the latest collection time are shown. Today is a partial day.
